🌪️ Natural Disasters

Select a disaster to view precautions and safety information

🌊 Flood

⚠️ Precautions

  • Move to higher ground if water levels are rising.
  • Avoid walking or driving through floodwater.
  • Stay away from fallen electrical wires.
  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Follow official evacuation instructions.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Stay in a safe elevated location.
  • Follow official emergency announcements.
  • Do not return until authorities say it is safe.

🌍 Earthquake

⚠️ Precautions

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On during shaking.
  • Stay away from windows and objects that may fall.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings and power lines.
  • Be prepared for aftershocks.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Protect yourself during the shaking.
  • Move carefully to a safe area after shaking stops.
  • Follow instructions from emergency authorities.

📞 Emergency Contact

🌀 Cyclone

⚠️ Precautions

  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Follow official weather warnings.
  • Keep water and essential supplies ready.
  • Follow evacuation orders when issued.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Stay indoors until the danger has passed.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings and fallen wires.
  • Follow official instructions.

📞 Emergency Contact

🌊 Tsunami

⚠️ Precautions

  • Move inland or to higher ground after an official warning.
  • Stay away from beaches and coastal areas.
  • Follow evacuation routes.
  • Do not return until authorities give the all-clear.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Follow official tsunami warnings.
  • Move to a safe elevated location.
  • Stay away from floodwater and damaged areas.

⛰️ Landslide

⚠️ Precautions

  • Stay away from unstable slopes.
  • Avoid areas where the ground is cracking or moving.
  • Stay away from landslide debris.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Move to a safe and stable area.
  • Do not enter affected areas unless authorities say it is safe.
  • Report dangerous conditions to authorities.

📞 Emergency Contact

⚡ Lightning / Thunderstorm

⚠️ Precautions

  • Go indoors or into a hard-topped vehicle.
  • Avoid isolated trees and open areas.
  • Stay away from water during thunderstorms.
  • Keep away from windows.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Remain indoors until the storm passes.
  • Avoid unnecessary outdoor travel.
  • Seek emergency help if someone is seriously injured.

📞 Emergency Contact

🔥 Forest Fire / Wildfire

⚠️ Precautions

  • Stay away from fire and heavy smoke.
  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Do not enter restricted fire-affected areas.
  • Keep windows and doors closed when smoke is heavy.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Move away from fire and smoke.
  • Follow official evacuation routes.
  • Contact emergency services if you are in immediate danger.

☀️ Extreme Heat

⚠️ Precautions

  • Stay in a cool or shaded place.
  • Drink fluids regularly.
  • Avoid strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day.
  • Follow local heat warnings.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Move to a cooler place if you feel unwell.
  • Seek medical help for serious heat-related symptoms.
  • Keep yourself hydrated.
