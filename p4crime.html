🏠 Theft / Robbery

⚠️ Precautions

  • Move to a safe location if a robbery is happening.
  • Do not confront or chase the person.
  • Keep important belongings secure.
  • Remember useful details only if it is safe to do so.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Contact the police as soon as you are safe.
  • Do not disturb possible evidence unnecessarily.
  • Tell a trusted adult or responsible person.
