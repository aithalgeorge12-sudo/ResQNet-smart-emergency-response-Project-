🚗 Accident Emergency

Get immediate help and report important accident information

⚠️ Immediate Precautions

  • Move to a safe place if there is continuing danger.
  • Do not move an injured person unnecessarily, especially after a serious crash.
  • Do not give food or drink to a seriously injured person.
  • Keep the area around the injured person clear.
  • Call emergency services as soon as possible.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.

📸 Accident Scene

If it is safe to do so, take a photo of the accident scene for emergency responders.
