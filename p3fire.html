🔥 Fire Emergency

Follow these safety measures during a fire emergency

⚠️ Immediate Precautions

  • Stay calm and alert others nearby about the fire.
  • Move away from the fire and smoke as quickly as possible.
  • Use the nearest safe exit.
  • Stay low if there is heavy smoke.
  • Never use an elevator during a building fire.
  • Follow the building's fire evacuation plan.

🛡️ What to Do

  • Raise the alarm and alert people nearby.
  • Call emergency services immediately.
  • Leave the building using a safe exit.
  • Close doors behind you if you can do so safely, without locking them.
  • Once outside, move to a safe assembly point.
  • Do not re-enter the building until authorities declare it safe.

🚫 What to Avoid

  • Do not go back inside for personal belongings.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Do not enter rooms filled with smoke.
  • Do not attempt to fight a large or rapidly spreading fire yourself.
  • Do not block emergency exits.

👕 If Clothes Catch Fire

  • Stop moving.
  • Drop to the ground.
  • Roll to help put out the flames.
  • Ask someone nearby to call emergency services.

🌫️ If There Is Heavy Smoke

  • Stay as low as possible.
  • Move toward a safe exit.
  • Avoid breathing unnecessary smoke.
  • If you cannot safely escape, stay in a protected location and signal for help if possible.

📞 Emergency Contacts

📍 Current Location

Share your location with emergency responders or a trusted person if it is safe to do so.
